Biography

I am a second-year Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Ophthalmology at the Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania. Supervised by Dr. Miller, my dissertation research focuses on functional neurocircuitry restoration in degenerative retinal diseases. Specifically, we harness targeted optogenetic tools and biomaterial scaffold interfaces to delay rod photoreceptor degeneration and explore potential clinical interventions for inherited night blindness (nyctalopia) .

I graduated with a B.S. in Biomedical Sciences from Boston University in 2023, where my undergraduate work in visual prosthetics laid the foundation for my current research. My ultimate goal is to bridge molecular engineering and ophthalmological translation.
